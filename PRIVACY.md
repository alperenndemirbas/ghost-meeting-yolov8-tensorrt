# Privacy Policy — Ghost Meeting

Ghost Meeting is a personal, non-commercial project that detects unused ("ghost") meeting room bookings.

## What the app accesses

- **Google Calendar (events only):** the app reads and manages events on one meeting-room calendar so it can tell whether a booked meeting has started, and release or create bookings on request.
- **Camera and microphone of the room:** images and audio are processed locally on the owner's own machine. They are never uploaded to any external service and are not stored.
- **Slack:** the app sends direct messages to meeting organizers and receives their button replies.

## Face data

Face recognition is optional and only runs for people who gave explicit consent. Face data stays on the local machine and can be deleted at any time with the `/verilerimi-sil` Slack command.

## Sharing

No personal data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. Google user data is used only to provide the features described above.
